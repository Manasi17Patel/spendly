Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Spendly collects, uses, and protects information when you use our personal expense tracking service. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

To provide expense tracking, Spendly collects the information you choose to enter, including expense amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions or notes you add. We also collect basic account information such as your email address and a hashed password so you can sign in securely.

Like most web services, we automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and device information. This is used to keep the service running reliably and to detect abuse.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate and improve Spendly — for example, to display your spending summaries, category breakdowns, and time-period reports. We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not use your expense entries to build advertising profiles.

We may use aggregated, non-identifying statistics (such as the total number of users in a given month) to understand how the service is used and to guide future development.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in secured databases accessible only to authorized systems and personnel. We use industry-standard safeguards — including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and hashed passwords — to help protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however. While we work to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account password confidential.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of trusted third parties to operate, such as hosting providers and email delivery services for account-related messages. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their function and are expected to handle it in line with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

We do not place third-party advertising trackers on Spendly, and we do not share your expense data with marketing partners.

5. Your Choices

You can review, update, or delete the expense entries in your account at any time through the app. You may also request a full export of your data or the closure of your account by contacting us using the details below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the service, our practices, or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised whenever changes are made. Continued use of Spendly after any such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided on our website. We will do our best to respond in a timely manner.
